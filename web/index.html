A detective game

Nobody Leaves

Locked-in murders. Four suspects. One accusation.

Every case traps a handful of people in one place for one night, and one of them is a killer. The suspects answer in their own words, and every one of them is hiding something. Search for evidence, catch the lies, and name the right person before your actions run out.

How it works

Rules of the house

  1. Ask the suspects anything. They answer in their own words.
  2. Search the place for evidence. Some of it stays hidden until a suspect gives you a reason to look.
  3. Show a suspect the exhibit that catches them out, and their story breaks.
  4. Every question, search and exhibit shown costs one action.
  5. You get one accusation: who, why, and the proof.

Case files

Pick a case. Each one is a separate mystery with its own suspects.
